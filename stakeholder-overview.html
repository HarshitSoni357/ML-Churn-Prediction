Project overview
A decision-support demonstration

Spotting possible customer inactivity

This project turns past customer activity into an estimate of whether a customer may become inactive in the next 30 days.

  1. 1

    Bring activity together

    Combine customer, order, payment, subscription, support, marketing, and feedback records.

    In this project: simulated data
  2. 2

    Build a weekly picture

    Summarize what was known about each customer at regular points in time.

    Recent behavior becomes useful signals
  3. 3

    Learn from the past

    Train and check prediction models using earlier and later time periods.

    Later dates are held back for testing
  4. 4

    Estimate inactivity risk

    For a customer, the service returns a probability of inactivity in the next 30 days.

    An estimate, not a certainty
  5. 5

    People choose what to do

    A team can consider the estimate alongside context and business priorities.

    No automatic customer action

Checks around the model

Historical time-based testing helps check how predictions behave on later dates. The API also exposes service health and request metrics. Data-shift analysis is an offline check, not a live automatic retraining system.

What this project does not claim

The data is synthetic. The results demonstrate an engineering workflow, not proven retention improvement or validated performance on real customers.
